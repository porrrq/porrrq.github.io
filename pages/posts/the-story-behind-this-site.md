## <a href="https://bearblog.dev/" target="_blank">ʕ•ᴥ•ʔ Bear Blog</a> is my inspiration

**I had been exploring some sites (e.g., Medium, Blogger) and none of them felt appealing**. I want something simple that I can control. 
One day I was scrolling through Facebook and I saw this <a href="https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/hacker-tube/" target="_blank">Karpathy's bearblog</a>. This site is particularly compelling as it showcases how simple a blog website can be.

At first, I signed in and was about to start my blog. Then, out of nowhere, this thought just popped up:

> Trying to make a blog website yourself is fun, isn't it?

Without further ado, I started to plan the project.

## Methodology
### Workflow

The workflow is relatively simple:

1. Write all blog posts in `.md`
2. Let JavaScript build an index and convert them to HTML content
3. Use *Codeberg pages* and *Codeberg actions* to host and automate the building process.

That's it! It's a simple static site generation workflow.

### Rules

AI enthusiasts might say 

> Why building it yourself, huh? Still coding by hand?
>
> Just prompting the AI to build this for you. It will instantly give you the site with an optimized workflow, bro.

Well, I think people do things for various reasons, and no reason is better than others. **AI gives you finished output that works, but you have no understanding of what's under the hood**. Consequently, you don't have the ability to maintain the code by yourself, or even apply small (or large) configurations to tailor the site to your needs, which constantly change. And **I think that's not fun at all**. From my perspective, coding and building something is fun because of the experience while you're struggling and figuring it out, and the sense of ownership that creates a secure feeling, which comes from an ability to maintain your project by yourself, not solely because of the output satisfaction. 

**The objective of building a side project should be fun!**
Don't constantly apply the corporate mindset, only focusing on output volume, to everything in your life. Otherwise you will eventually transform into a *soulless machine*.

So, I decided to create some rules:
+ Minimize AI usage and don't mindlessly copy and paste. I have to understand the code.
+ Write code with my very own hands!
+ No JavaScript frameworks allowed ("Just of fun!")

That's all.
